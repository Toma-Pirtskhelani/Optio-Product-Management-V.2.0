## 1. Spine

Every row carries the spine from `schemas/merged-table.md` § 1: `pass_id`,
`raw_name`, `normalized_name`, `source_url` (row-level), `rung`, `retrieved_at`,
`snapshot_date`, `source_lang`, `grade`, `notes`.

## 2. Enumeration rule

Categories come from G2's own published category index — fetched, at R1, with
the index URL and the count G2 itself states recorded in the pass header. Never
from recall of what G2 categories exist (P1). If the index paginates, every page
is fetched and logged; a partial enumeration is declared partial in
`scope`.

**Rung discipline specific to this source:** a G2 profile page is `R1` for
*"G2 lists this vendor in this category"* and `R3` for anything about the vendor
itself — headcount, pricing, customers, HQ. Vendor facts require a separate R1
fetch of the vendor's own site or filing, with its own fetch-log row. Recording a
vendor fact at `PRIMARY` because a G2 page was fetched at R1 is exactly the
grade-inflation `CLAUDE.md` prohibits.

## 5. Failure sampling (P4) — mechanisms for this source

Review platforms delete the dead. Named mechanisms, all logged even when empty:

1. `web.archive.org` snapshots of each category page at ≥12 and ≥24 month
   intervals; diff vendor lists; anything present then and absent now is a
   candidate. Record as `DELISTED`, not yet as failed.
2. Resolve each candidate: fetch its own domain at R1. Dead domain, parked
   domain, redirect to an acquirer, or a shutdown notice each produce a distinct
   `outcome` in `outputs/failure-register.md`.
3. Never infer failure from delisting alone. Delisting also happens on
   rebranding, category migration, and non-payment. `UNKNOWN` until resolved.

A pass with zero failure candidates must attach its archive-diff log.

## 6. Language protocol (P5)

Record every locale of the category index fetched. If scope includes a
non-anglophone market, fetch that market's locale pages and log the in-language
queries verbatim. An all-`en` G2 pass may be a correct result for this source —
but that result belongs in `outputs/coverage-report.md` as a *blindness of the
source*, not as evidence that local vendors don't exist.

## 7. Rejection

Standard checklist, `research-protocol.md` § 7, plus:

- [ ] Enumeration came from a fetched index, with G2's own stated count logged.
- [ ] `captured_count` vs `stated_count` gap explained.
- [ ] No vendor fact graded `PRIMARY` on the strength of a G2 page alone.
- [ ] Archive diff run for failure sampling, log attached.
- [ ] `parent_path` captured verbatim — it is the raw material for the
      granularity conflicts Table A exists to expose.
