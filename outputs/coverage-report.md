# outputs/coverage-report.md

What this method structurally cannot see.

This file ships **with** the merged table, not after it. Its job is to stop a
reader concluding that the merged table is the market, when it is four
catalogues of the market with correlated inclusion rules. V1 had no equivalent
file, which is why its Gartner skew went uncorrected for the life of the project.

Populated from Table C in `schemas/merged-table.md` § 4. Every `structural_blindness`
claim needs a mechanism and a URL — a published inclusion criterion, a listing
fee, a filter definition. **"Probably underrepresented" is not a finding.**

## Dimensions to test first

Not a finished list — these are the hypotheses the source set's known mechanics
suggest, each to be **verified or refuted against the sources' own published
rules**, not assumed:

- Vendors that never raised outside capital.
- Vendors with no English-language web presence.
- Vendors selling direct/on-prem with no marketplace or review-platform presence.
- Services firms and consultancies selling the same outcome without software.
- In-house builds and spreadsheets — frequently the largest real competitor, and
  invisible to all four sources by construction.
- Dead companies, per source.
- Vendors below analyst revenue floors.
- The pay-to-play question: for each source, does prominence measure market
  position or marketing spend? Answer from the source's own published ranking
  basis, quoted.
