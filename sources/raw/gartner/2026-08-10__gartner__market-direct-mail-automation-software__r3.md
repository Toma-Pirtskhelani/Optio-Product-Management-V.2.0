What is Direct Mail Automation Software?
Direct mail automation software automates the process of creating, managing, and sending physical mail campaigns. It can include letters, brochures, catalogs, postcards, gifts, and such. It enables marketers to segment target audiences, personalize mail pieces, automate the sending process, and track delivery and response rates. It typically integrates with customer relationship management (CRM) systems to enable efficient direct mail communications.
