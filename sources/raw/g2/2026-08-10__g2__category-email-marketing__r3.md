Email marketing software allows businesses to build and manage email lists, segment audiences, design and send customized campaigns, and monitor subscriber engagement, helping marketing teams, growth teams, and small business owners engage prospects, nurture leads, and retain customers through personalized communication.

Core Capabilities of Email Marketing Software
To qualify for inclusion in the Email Marketing category, a product must:

Enable the creation and sending of emails via HTML or a WYSIWYG editor
Provide email templates
Allow users to preview and send test emails
Store, track, segment, and manage email contact lists
Offer campaign-based reporting and analytics to measure engagement and performance
Support clear opt-in and easy opt-out options compliant with global regulations such as CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and CASL
Focus on email as the primary channel and not be categorized as marketing automation
Common Use Cases for Email Marketing Software
Email marketing tools are used across industries to drive direct communication with prospects and customers. Common use cases include:

Sending targeted promotional campaigns and newsletters to segmented subscriber lists
Triggering automated emails based on customer behavior and CRM data
Running drip campaigns to nurture leads through the sales funnel
How Email Marketing Software Differs from Other Tools
This category covers point solutions dedicated to email as the primary channel. While email marketing is a common feature within marketing automation platforms, those tools go beyond email by offering lead management, dynamic segmentation, landing pages, and full campaign lifecycle analytics. Some email marketing tools extend their reach with SMS marketing, push notifications, or social media management capabilities via integrations.

Insights from G2 on Email Marketing Software
Based on category trends on G2, drag-and-drop email builders, list segmentation, and campaign analytics stand out as the most valued features. These platforms deliver ease of use and strong deliverability rates as primary reasons for platform selection.

Learn More About Email Marketing Software
Email marketing software buying insights at a glance
Email marketing software helps businesses create, send, and optimize email campaigns at scale. It’s a core part of the marketing stack, used not only for newsletters but also for lifecycle messaging, promotional campaigns, and customer engagement. Modern tools combine campaign builders, audience segmentation, automation workflows, and analytics to help teams communicate with large audiences while keeping messaging targeted and relevant.

Organizations adopt email marketing solutions to manage recurring communication efficiently and maintain consistent engagement with customers and prospects. Most platforms provide drag-and-drop email editors, contact management, campaign scheduling, and performance tracking. Pricing models usually scale with subscriber counts or sending volume, meaning small teams can start affordably and expand capabilities as their audience grows.

Businesses typically use email marketing software to run newsletters, product announcements, and promotional campaigns quickly without relying on complex design or development resources. Many teams also use these tools to automate follow-up sequences and lifecycle email programs so leads and customers receive timely communication based on their actions or stage in the journey. 

Another major benefit is the ability to segment contact lists, which allows marketers to send more relevant messages to different audience groups rather than broadcasting the same campaign to everyone. 

Email marketing platforms also help teams track engagement metrics such as opens, clicks, and conversions, making it easier to evaluate campaign performance and refine messaging over time.

Support can also feel slow depending on the plan, so when something breaks, you end up troubleshooting more than you want.” - Diego A., Intuit Mailchimp Email Marketing review

My Expert Takeaway on Email Marketing Software in 2026
Email marketing remains one of the most consistent channels for customer engagement, but the value teams get from these tools depends largely on how well they operationalize campaigns. Across this category, the software averages 4.49 stars and an 8.98 likelihood to recommend, suggesting strong overall satisfaction among buyers.

High-performing teams typically use email marketing software as a structured communication engine rather than an occasional campaign tool. They rely on segmentation, automation workflows, and performance tracking to continuously refine messaging. From what I observed, marketing and technology companies make up a large share of reviewers, reflecting how frequently these industries rely on repeatable outreach and nurture programs.

Another pattern is that smaller and mid-sized organizations benefit the most from platforms that balance capability with simplicity. Teams that prioritize intuitive editors, flexible segmentation, and clear reporting tend to see faster campaign cycles and better engagement over time. In practice, the best email marketing platforms are the ones that remove operational friction while still giving marketers enough control to experiment, personalize campaigns, and scale communication as their audience grows.

Email Marketing Software FAQs
What is the best email marketing app for Shopify?
If your business runs on Shopify, the best email marketing tools are those that sync directly with your store data, such as orders, customer profiles, and abandoned carts.

Common options include:

Intuit Mailchimp Email Marketing: A widely used platform that integrates with Shopify to manage subscriber lists, run campaigns, and automate emails triggered by customer activity.
Constant Contact: A beginner-friendly email marketing platform that supports e-commerce integrations and helps businesses send promotional emails, newsletters, and automated follow-ups.
Brevo Marketing Platform: It connects with e-commerce systems to automate campaigns, manage customer contacts, and track engagement across email marketing programs.
How do I choose the best email marketing software?
When comparing email marketing tools, buyers usually evaluate how quickly teams can launch campaigns and automate outreach without adding operational complexity.

Key evaluation criteria include:

Campaign creation: Drag-and-drop builders and reusable templates
Automation: Capable of running lifecycle campaigns, nurture sequences, and triggered emails
Segmentation: Target audiences based on behavior or engagement
Analytics: Reporting on opens, clicks, and conversions
Integrations: Compatibility with CRM, e-commerce, and marketing tools
Why use email marketing software?
Email marketing software helps businesses manage campaigns, automate follow-ups, and track engagement from a single platform.

Teams typically use it to:

Run newsletters and promotional campaigns consistently
Automate welcome emails and nurture sequences
Segment audiences for targeted messaging
Track metrics like opens, clicks, and campaign performance
Instead of manually sending messages, marketers can schedule campaigns and automate communication across the customer lifecycle.

Which email marketing software integrates with CRM?
Many platforms integrate with CRM systems, enabling marketers to trigger campaigns based on customer data such as lifecycle stage, sales activity, or engagement history.

Common examples include:

Intuit Mailchimp Email Marketing: Integrates with CRM platforms such as Zoho, enabling teams to sync contacts and trigger campaigns based on engagement data.
Constant Contact: Connects with CRM systems like Salesforce and HubSpot, enabling businesses to sync customer contact lists and run targeted email campaigns.
Brevo Marketing Platform: Offers built-in CRM functionality alongside email marketing tools, allowing businesses to manage contacts, track customer interactions, and automate campaigns from the same platform.
What are some affordable email marketing software options for startups?
Startups often look for tools that offer core campaign and automation features without high entry costs.

Budget-friendly options include:

Brevo Marketing Platform: Combines email marketing, automation, and CRM features with flexible entry plans.
Moosend: Known for simple campaign builders and automation workflows suited for smaller teams.
MailerLite: Beginner-friendly platform with templates, landing pages, and basic automation for growing startups.
